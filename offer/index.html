AI Feature Sprint: Tuesday October 20 and Tuesday November 3 kickoffs AI Feature Sprint

Last updated Wednesday September 23

Here's what we're doing.

In ten business days I ship one working AI workflow into your product. A workflow with a clear input, a clear output, and a person on your team who uses it. It runs on your data, in your codebase, deployed to staging or delivered as a pull request your engineers can merge. No deck and no demo video.

You know AI has to be in the product this year. The roadmap is full, and nobody on the team has taken an AI feature to production. This is the shortest honest path from "we should do something with AI" to "it's live."

What you have on Day 10

  • The workflow, in staging or production, or as a production-ready pull request in your repo.
  • A recorded walkthrough of the workflow and the decisions behind it.
  • A scoring rubric your team uses to judge output quality, and a playbook they use to maintain and extend the workflow without me.
  • A 30-day roadmap and a 60 to 90 minute handoff session with your engineers.
  • 30 days of async support after handoff. Something breaks in week three, you write, I fix.

How the ten days run

Day 1 we scope: one workflow, one user, the input, the output, and the acceptance criteria, written into a Sprint Brief you sign off by email. Days 2 and 3 are design. Days 4 and 5 produce a working prototype on your real data, and you watch it run on Day 5. Days 6 to 9 are edge cases, error states, logging and prompt tuning. Day 10 is handoff.

Who it's for

Founders and CEOs of B2B SaaS companies with 5 to 50 people and paying customers, who have a workflow in mind (or three, and want help choosing), and whose team can give me codebase and data access plus one decision-maker who answers within a business day.

Who it's not for

Anyone who wants to "add AI" without a specific workflow, needs a full AI transformation, has data that isn't accessible, wants a general chatbot, or needs guaranteed production deployment regardless of their infrastructure.

Price

Guarantee

No working prototype on your data by Day 5? Stop the Sprint and get a full refund. The workflow in your signed Brief isn't running to its acceptance criteria by Day 10? Full refund. The Brief is the contract, which is why Day 1 is the most important day of the ten.
